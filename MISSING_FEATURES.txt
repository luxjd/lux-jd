  1. BROKER API INTEGRATIONS
================================================================================

  Source:     luxury_auto_arbitrage_1.docx §6.2.2
  Agent:      JP Sourcing Agent (#2)
  Priority:   LOW (Phase 3+)
  Status:     NOT IMPLEMENTED

  WHAT IT IS:
  Direct API integrations with Japanese vehicle broker services:
  - SBI Motor Japan (sbimotor.co.jp) — luxury vehicle export specialist
  - Be Forward (beforward.jp) — large-scale vehicle exporter
  - Other brokers: Autorec, Qualitex Trading, STC Japan

  PURPOSE:
  Brokers offer fixed-price purchases (no auction competition). They handle
  export documentation, pre-shipment inspection, and sometimes domestic
  transport. Their APIs would provide structured vehicle data feeds with
  standardized fields (price, specs, photos, condition reports).

  USE CASE:
  - Find vehicles that aren't listed at public auctions
  - Get fixed prices instead of competing at auction (lower risk)
  - Access pre-inspected inventory with broker's condition guarantees
  - Some brokers specialize in specific brands (e.g., luxury European)

  WHY IT'S DEFERRED:
  1. Current sources already cover 95%+ of available luxury inventory:
     - japan-auktion.de aggregator covers 12 auction houses (USS, TAA,
       HAA, JU, BH Auction, Arai, ZIP, CAA, AUCNET, MIRIVE, etc.)
     - goo-net.com: 500,000+ dealer listings
     - carsensor.net: 400,000+ dealer listings
  2. Broker APIs require business agreements (contracts, deposits,
     sometimes membership fees of ¥100,000-500,000/year)
  3. Be Forward primarily serves African/emerging market exports —
     limited luxury European inventory for the German market
  4. At 1-5 vehicles/month (Phase 1-2), inventory is not the bottleneck
  5. No broker offers a public API — each requires negotiation

  WHEN TO ADD:
  - Phase 3+ (month 9+), when processing 5-10 vehicles/month
  - When you've established relationships with specific brokers
  - When you want fixed-price sourcing to reduce auction risk
  - When a specific broker has inventory you can't find at auctions

  EFFORT TO IMPLEMENT:
  - Per broker: 2-3 days (API integration + normalization)
  - Requires: API key/credentials, documentation, test account
  - Architecture: add new scraper in src/lib/agents/jp-sourcing/scrapers/
    following the same interface as goo-net.js and carsensor.js

  RISK OF NOT HAVING:
  LOW — You might miss 5-10% of available inventory, but the current
  3-source setup provides more candidates than you can process.
  The 95 candidates found per scan proves inventory is abundant.

  2. PROVENANCE VERIFICATION SYSTEM
================================================================================

  Source:     luxury_auto_arbitrage_1.docx §6.2.3
  Agent:      JP Sourcing Agent (#2)
  Priority:   MEDIUM (Phase 2+ for Ferraris, Phase 3+ for others)
  Status:     PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED (basic checks only)

  WHAT IT IS:
  Deep verification of a vehicle's authenticity, originality, and history
  beyond what the auction sheet provides. Four sub-components:

  a) MATCHING NUMBERS VERIFICATION
     - Confirm engine number matches chassis/VIN records
     - For Ferraris: engine, gearbox, and chassis must all match
     - A re-engined Ferrari loses 30-50% of value
     - A non-matching Porsche loses 10-20%
     - Not relevant for Mercedes-AMG, BMW M, Bentley (engine swaps
       are extremely rare and don't impact value the same way)

  b) ORIGINAL COLOR VERIFICATION
     - Compare current color against factory build sheet records
     - A repainted Ferrari in non-original color loses 10-20%
     - Original-color vehicles command premiums, especially in
       factory-special colors (Blu Pozzi, Verde British Racing)
     - Already partially covered: our extraction detects 色替
       (color change arrow) on auction sheets

  c) SERVICE HISTORY COMPLETENESS
     - Verify claimed service history against manufacturer records
     - Confirm dealer stamps are authentic (not forged)
     - Check for gaps in the service timeline
     - Already partially covered: we extract service record entries
       with dates + mileage from auction sheets (P0 feature)

  d) LIMITED EDITION NUMBERING
     - For numbered editions (e.g., "Ferrari 488 Pista #234 of 500")
     - Verify the production number is real and matches the VIN
     - Fake limited edition claims exist in the market
     - Affects value by 5-15% for genuine limited editions

  PURPOSE:
  Prevent buying a vehicle whose value is significantly less than estimated
  due to hidden authenticity issues. The margin on a €200k Ferrari can
  evaporate if it turns out to be re-engined or repainted.

  USE CASE:
  - Ferrari purchases above €150k (matching numbers critical)
  - Lamborghini limited editions (edition numbering)
  - Any vehicle where the seller claims "original" condition
  - Detecting sophisticated fraud (forged service books, cloned VINs)

  WHAT'S ALREADY IMPLEMENTED:
  - Auction grade extraction (tells you overall condition, 100% accuracy)
  - Service book present/absent detection (100% accuracy)
  - Accident history extraction from auction sheet
  - Color change detection (色替 arrow on sheet)
  - Service record history parsing (dates + mileage entries)
  - VIN extraction and WMI validation
  - AI thesis flags "missing documentation" as a risk factor
  - Provenance risk score (FULL_DEALER/PARTIAL/UNKNOWN)

  WHY IT'S DEFERRED:
  1. Manufacturer databases are NOT publicly accessible:
     - Ferrari Classiche: in-person only, €500+ per lookup, 6+ month wait
     - Porsche PAP: authorized dealer access only
     - Mercedes Star Diagnostics: dealer credentials required
     - BMW ISTA: dealer license required
  2. No manufacturer offers a public API for vehicle history
  3. Manual verification is more reliable and cheaper for low volume:
     - Call a Ferrari dealer: €500, 10 minutes, definitive answer
     - Request build sheet from manufacturer: €50-100, 2-4 weeks
  4. Your bread-and-butter vehicles (AMG GT, 911, Cayman GT4, M3)
     are NOT numbers-matching sensitive — this issue is Ferrari-specific
  5. The auction grade from USS/TAA IS professional third-party verification

  WHEN TO ADD:
  - When you start buying Ferraris above €150k regularly (>2/month)
  - When a manufacturer opens API access (unlikely near-term)
  - When you partner with a dealer who can run checks for you
  - Phase 3+ as a manual workflow tool (track verification requests)

  EFFORT TO IMPLEMENT:
  - Automated: IMPOSSIBLE without manufacturer API access
  - Semi-automated (tracking system): 3-5 days
    - Create a "provenance check request" workflow
    - Track: requested → in-progress → verified/failed
    - Store results linked to vehicle record
    - Manual entry of Classiche results, build sheet data
  - Full integration: requires authorized dealer partnership

  RISK OF NOT HAVING:
  MEDIUM for Ferraris, LOW for other brands.
  Mitigation: avoid Ferraris in Phase 1, or manually verify (€500/check).
  The AI thesis already flags documentation gaps, and human review
  catches this before "Approve Bid" is clicked.

  3. VIN CROSS-REFERENCE WITH MANUFACTURER DATABASES
================================================================================

  Source:     luxury_auto_arbitrage_1.docx §6.2.3
  Agent:      JP Sourcing Agent (#2)
  Priority:   LOW (Phase 3+)
  Status:     PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED (format validation only)

  WHAT IT IS:
  Query vehicle manufacturer service databases using the 17-character VIN
  to retrieve:
  - Complete factory specification (every option code)
  - Full dealer service history (dates, mileage, work performed)
  - Outstanding recalls or campaigns
  - Warranty status and coverage remaining
  - Original delivery market and date
  - Odometer readings at each dealer visit (detects rollback)

  PURPOSE:
  Provides "ground truth" verification independent of the seller's claims.
  If the auction sheet says "62,000 km" but the manufacturer database shows
  the last dealer visit at 78,000 km, the odometer has been rolled back.
  If the seller claims "full dealer service" but the database shows gaps,
  the service books may be forged.

  USE CASE:
  - Odometer fraud detection (rare in Japan, but exists)
  - Service history verification (confirm dealer stamps are real)
  - Specification verification (confirm options actually installed)
  - Recall status (unfixed recalls = liability + cost)
  - Warranty transfer eligibility (some warranties transfer, adding value)

  WHAT'S ALREADY IMPLEMENTED:
  - VIN extraction from auction sheets (69% accuracy, improving)
  - VIN format validation (17 chars, valid charset, check digit)
  - WMI prefix validation (VIN prefix matches stated brand)
  - NHTSA VIN decoder (US database — limited but free, validates
    make/model/year/engine against the VIN)
  - Service record extraction from auction sheets (dates + mileage)

  WHY IT'S DEFERRED:
  1. NO manufacturer offers public VIN lookup APIs:
     - Ferrari: dealer-only portal (FerrariNet)
     - Porsche: dealer-only (PIWIS/PAP)
     - Mercedes: dealer-only (XENTRY/Star Diagnostics)
     - BMW: dealer-only (ISTA)
     - Bentley: dealer-only (ODIS)
     - Aston Martin: dealer-only
     - Jaguar/Land Rover: dealer-only (SDD/PATHFINDER)
  2. Third-party services have limited Japan coverage:
     - Carfax: primarily US/Canada, minimal Japan data
     - AutoCheck: US-focused
     - HPI Check: UK-focused
     - Japan Automobile Inspection Association: not accessible to foreigners
  3. Building scrapers for dealer portals would violate terms of service
     and potentially be illegal (computer fraud laws)
  4. The NHTSA decoder (already integrated) covers basic validation
  5. Japanese auction houses (USS, TAA) perform their own VIN verification
     as part of the inspection process — the auction grade implicitly
     includes VIN authenticity verification

  WHEN TO ADD:
  - When a third-party service launches Japan VIN history coverage
  - When you partner with an authorized dealer who can batch-query
  - When processing 10+ vehicles/month (justifies the partnership cost)
  - Consider a manual workflow: for each BUY candidate, email the
    brand's dealer with the VIN and request a history printout

  EFFORT TO IMPLEMENT:
  - Automated: IMPOSSIBLE without dealer API access
  - Manual workflow: 1-2 days (request tracking system)
  - Third-party integration (if service exists): 2-3 days per service

  RISK OF NOT HAVING:
  LOW — Japanese auction houses verify VINs during inspection. The auction
  grade IS your VIN verification. Odometer fraud is extremely rare in Japan
  (strict Shaken system records mileage biennially). The bigger risk is
  buying a vehicle with undisclosed repairs, which the auction sheet's
  修復歴 (repair history) field already covers.

  4. HAGERTY / CLASSIC ANALYTICS MARKET INDICES
================================================================================

  Source:     luxury_auto_arbitrage_1.docx §6.1.2
  Agent:      DE Market Agent (#1)
  Priority:   LOW (Phase 3+)
  Status:     NOT IMPLEMENTED

  WHAT IT IS:
  Integration with collector car valuation services:
  - Hagerty Valuation Tools: condition-graded valuations for collector
    vehicles (#1 Concours, #2 Excellent, #3 Good, #4 Fair)
  - Classic Analytics: European market indices tracking appreciation/
    depreciation of specific models over time

  PURPOSE:
  Provides long-term trend data and condition-specific valuations for
  vehicles that are transitioning from "used car" to "collector car"
  status. A Ferrari 488 is currently a used sports car, but in 5-10
  years it becomes a collectible — Hagerty tracks this transition.

  USE CASE:
  - Identifying models that are starting to appreciate (buy before
    the market moves — e.g., manual-transmission 997.2 GT3)
  - Setting floor prices for vehicles that are below market but
    above collector value (protect against overpaying)
  - Long-term portfolio strategy (hold vs. sell decisions)
  - Marketing angle: "Hagerty values this at €X" adds credibility
    to listings on classicdriver/elferspot

  WHY IT'S DEFERRED:
  1. Subscription cost: Hagerty Pro is $300-500/year, Classic Analytics
     is €400-800/year
  2. Limited API access — Hagerty offers basic lookups but not bulk data
  3. Your target vehicles (2016-2023 models) are too new for most
     collector indices — they track vehicles 10+ years old
  4. Your DE Market Agent's live scraping of mobile.de/AutoScout24
     provides MORE ACCURATE current pricing than static indices
  5. Indices are updated monthly/quarterly — your data is hourly

  WHEN TO ADD:
  - When you start dealing in pre-2015 vehicles (genuine collector territory)
  - When you need "official" valuation references for insurance or financing
  - Phase 3+ as a supplementary data source for the DE Market Agent

  EFFORT TO IMPLEMENT:
  - Hagerty API integration: 1-2 days
  - Classic Analytics: manual data entry (they don't have an API)
  - Would add a new data source to the TVR generator

  RISK OF NOT HAVING:
  NEGLIGIBLE for current operations. Your real-time scraping is better
  than monthly indices for 2016-2023 model years.

  5. CONFIDENCE CALIBRATION (ACTUAL vs PREDICTED)
================================================================================

  Source:     valuation_agent_spec.docx §7.2 (Nice to Have)
  Agent:      Valuation Agent (#0)
  Priority:   MEDIUM (ongoing from first sale)
  Status:     INFRASTRUCTURE BUILT, NEEDS DATA

  WHAT IT IS:
  A feedback loop that compares the agent's predicted sale price against
  the actual sale price when a vehicle is sold. Over time, this measures
  and improves prediction accuracy.

  WHAT'S ALREADY BUILT:
  - ValuationCalibration Prisma model in database schema
  - POST /api/agents/valuation/calibration — submit actual sale price
  - GET /api/agents/valuation/calibration — accuracy statistics
  - Computes: MAE (Mean Absolute Error), MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage
    Error), bias direction (over/under-predicting)

  WHAT'S MISSING:
  - Actual sales data (you haven't sold any vehicles yet)
  - UI for submitting calibration data (currently API-only)
  - Automated feedback when Finance Agent records a sale
  - Dashboard showing prediction accuracy over time

  PURPOSE:
  Without calibration, you don't know if the agent is consistently
  over-predicting (dangerous — you'd buy overpriced vehicles) or
  under-predicting (conservative — you'd miss opportunities).

  WHEN TO ACTIVATE:
  - After your first vehicle sale — submit the actual price via API
  - After 5 sales: enough data for basic statistics
  - After 20 sales: reliable MAPE calculation
  - Wire Finance Agent sale completion → auto-submit calibration

  EFFORT TO COMPLETE:
  - UI form for manual calibration entry: 1 day
  - Auto-submit from Finance Agent: 0.5 days
  - Dashboard visualization: 1 day

  RISK OF NOT HAVING:
  MEDIUM — you're flying blind on prediction accuracy until you close
  the feedback loop. But the first few sales will be manually managed
  anyway, so you'll know intuitively if predictions are close.

  8. SHEET EXTRACTION ACCURACY → 95%+
================================================================================

  Source:     Implied by valuation_agent_spec.docx §4.3.2
  Agent:      Valuation Agent (#0)
  Priority:   MEDIUM (ongoing)
  Status:     88.6% ACCURACY (target: 95%+)

  WHAT'S BLOCKING 95%:
  - VIN: 67% — needs region cropping (crop VIN row, upscale, multi-model)
  - registrationPlate: 50% — needs region cropping
  - Handwritten sheets: ~60% — needs specialized OCR or region cropping
  - bodyType: 42% → 91% (fixed with reverse mapping)

  WHAT'S NEEDED:
  1. Region cropping pipeline (Stage A from the pipeline spec):
     - Crop each field zone from the sheet image
     - Upscale to ~2000px per zone
     - Read each zone independently
     - Already proven for mileage (zone extractor → 100% accuracy)
  2. 50+ calibration sheets for template calibration:
     - 13 sheets is too small for reliable per-field statistics
     - Need USS + TAA + HAA + AUCNET formats
     - Need typed + handwritten + mixed
  3. VIN zone extractor calibration:
     - Built but disabled (locator crops wrong region sometimes)
     - Needs bounding box calibration on more sheets

  WHEN TO ADD:
  - Region cropping: when you provide 50+ calibration sheets
  - VIN zone: after locator calibration (needs 20+ VIN examples)
  - Ongoing: each new sheet type encountered → add to calibration set

  EFFORT: 3-5 days for region cropping pipeline, ongoing calibration
  RISK OF NOT HAVING: MEDIUM — 88.6% means ~1 in 9 fields wrong.
  Human review catches errors before they affect purchases.
