  AGENT #0 — VALUATION AGENT
================================================================================

  MISSING 1: Sheet Extraction Accuracy → 95%+
  ────────────────────────────────────────────
  Status:     88.6% ACCURACY (target: 95%+)
  Priority:   MEDIUM (ongoing)
  Source:     valuation_agent_spec.docx §4.3.2

  What's blocking 95%:
  - VIN: 67% — needs region cropping (crop VIN row, upscale, multi-model)
  - registrationPlate: 50% — needs region cropping
  - Handwritten sheets: ~60% — needs specialized OCR pipeline
  - bodyType: 42% → 91% (already fixed with reverse mapping)

  What's needed:
  1. Region cropping pipeline (Stage A from pipeline spec):
     - Crop each field zone from the sheet image
     - Upscale to ~2000px per zone
     - Read each zone independently
     - Already proven for mileage (zone extractor → 100% accuracy)
  2. 50+ calibration sheets (need USS + TAA + HAA + AUCNET formats)
  3. VIN zone extractor calibration (built but disabled)

  When to add: When 50+ calibration sheets are provided
  Effort: 3-5 days for region cropping, ongoing calibration
  Risk of not having: MEDIUM — 88.6% means ~1 in 9 fields wrong.
  Human review catches errors before they affect purchases.

  MISSING 4: LLM Cost vs Budget ($0.45 vs $0.10 target)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Status:     OVER BUDGET (4-5x)
  Priority:   LOW
  Source:     valuation_agent_spec.docx §8.2

  Current cost: ~$0.35-0.60/sheet extraction, ~$0.80-1.20/full valuation
  Spec target: ~$0.10/valuation

  Why over budget: Multi-pass extraction (3 passes + 8 verification calls +
  zone extractor + Opus verification) is 10-15x more API calls than the
  spec assumed (1 pass). The accuracy improvement (69.6% → 88.6%) justifies
  the cost — a wrong purchase decision on a €100k vehicle costs far more
  than $0.50 in API calls.

  What could reduce cost:
  - Skip deep parse when Tier 1 confident (IMPLEMENTED, saves ~$0.35)
  - Use Haiku for low-stakes calls (enrichment, classification)
  - Accept lower accuracy for lower cost (not recommended)

  Risk of not having: NEGLIGIBLE — $0.50/valuation at 100 vehicles/month
  = $50/month. The margin on one vehicle covers years of API costs.

  MISSING 7: Provenance Verification System
  ─────────────────────────────────────────
  Status:     PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED (basic checks only)
  Priority:   MEDIUM (Phase 2+ for Ferraris)
  Source:     luxury_auto_arbitrage_1.docx §6.2.3

  Four sub-components:
  a) Matching numbers (engine/chassis) — Ferrari-specific, critical
  b) Original color vs build sheet — partially covered (色替 detection)
  c) Service history completeness — partially covered (record parsing)
  d) Limited edition numbering — not implemented

  Why deferred:
  - Manufacturer databases NOT publicly accessible (Ferrari Classiche,
    Porsche PAP, Mercedes XENTRY — all dealer-only)
  - No manufacturer offers a public API
  - Manual verification is cheaper for low volume (€500/Ferrari check)
  - Bread-and-butter vehicles (AMG GT, 911, M3) are NOT numbers-matching sensitive

  When to add: When buying Ferraris above €150k regularly (>2/month)
  Effort: Automated = IMPOSSIBLE. Semi-automated tracking: 3-5 days.
  Risk of not having: MEDIUM for Ferraris, LOW for other brands


  MISSING 8: VIN Cross-Reference with Manufacturer Databases
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Status:     PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED (format validation + NHTSA decoder)
  Priority:   LOW (Phase 3+)
  Source:     luxury_auto_arbitrage_1.docx §6.2.3

  What it would provide: factory spec, full service history, recalls,
  warranty status, odometer readings at dealer visits

  Why deferred:
  - NO manufacturer offers public VIN lookup APIs (all dealer-only portals)
  - Third-party services (Carfax, AutoCheck) have minimal Japan coverage
  - Building scrapers for dealer portals would violate terms of service
  - Japanese auction houses verify VINs during inspection (auction grade = VIN verified)
  - Odometer fraud is extremely rare in Japan (Shaken records mileage biennially)

  When to add: When a third-party launches Japan VIN coverage, or dealer partnership
  Effort: Automated = IMPOSSIBLE without dealer API access. Manual workflow: 1-2 days.
  Risk of not having: LOW — auction grade IS your VIN verification
